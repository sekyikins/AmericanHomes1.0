# AmericanHomes — User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Test Plan

**Purpose:** Structured UAT test plan for the Business Owner (`Mr. Zero`) and intended warehouse/operations/finance staff.  
**Environment:** Isolated Staging/UAT environment (`apps/web` + `apps/api` + Staging PostgreSQL).  
**Data Rule:** Use synthetic test customers, test phone numbers, and Paystack test/stub mode only. Do **not** use real customer financial data.

---

## UAT Scenarios

| ID | Workflow Area | User Role | Preconditions | Test Data | Steps to Execute | Expected Business Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UAT-01** | Staff Login, MFA & Password Change | Staff Member | Active staff account created by Owner | `qa.staff@americanhomes.gh` / temp password | 1. Go to `/staff/login` and sign in.<br>2. Click name in header -> **Change password**.<br>3. Sign in with new password. | Login succeeds; old sessions are invalidated; new password works immediately. |
| **UAT-02** | Customer Registration & OTP Sign-In | Customer | Unregistered personal phone number | `+233200000099`, OTP `123456` (dummy mode) | 1. Open Storefront -> **Sign in** -> **Create account**.<br>2. Enter name, phone, OTP `123456`.<br>3. Open **Account settings -> Security -> Sign out of all other sessions**. | Customer account created; header shows customer name; other sessions revoked while current tab stays signed in. |
| **UAT-03** | Shipment, Receiving & Discrepancy Gate | Warehouse Staff | Staff has `shipments.*` and `receiving.*` | Shipment `SHP-UAT-01`, Manifest qty `5`, Received qty `3` | 1. Create shipment (`PLANNED`) and manifest (`5` units).<br>2. Move status `IN_TRANSIT` -> `ARRIVED`.<br>3. Start receiving and record `3` units.<br>4. Try closing shipment.<br>5. Resolve discrepancy (`SHORT_SHIPPED_CONFIRMED`) and close. | Receiving creates `MISSING (2)` discrepancy & `3` `Unavailable` stock units; closing is blocked until discrepancy is resolved. |
| **UAT-04** | Onboarding, Pricing & Publication | Prep Staff & Manager/Owner | `3` `Unavailable` units exist | Product `"UAT Sofa"`, Price `GHS 3,500`, Defect `"Minor scuff on back leg"` | 1. Try **Photography** before **Cleaning**.<br>2. Complete Inspection -> Cleaning -> Photography -> Wrapping in order.<br>3. Propose price `3500`.<br>4. Second manager/Owner approves price and publishes. | Out-of-order step is blocked; self-approval by non-owner is blocked; published product appears on `/shop` with `3` available units. |
| **UAT-05** | Stocktake & Adjustment Approval | Inventory Staff & Manager | Product has `3` available units | Counted qty `2`, Reason `"Damaged in bay"` | 1. Open `/staff/stocktakes`, create stocktake and enter count `2`.<br>2. Propose `-1` adjustment.<br>3. Second manager/Owner approves adjustment. | Available stock decreases to `2` with an `InventoryMovement` and `AuditEvent` recorded. |
| **UAT-06** | Product Request & Withdrawal Cutoff | Customer & Sourcing Staff | Customer signed in | Request `"Dyson V15"`, Qty `1` | 1. Customer submits `/request-product`.<br>2. Customer withdraws a second test request before supplier confirmation.<br>3. Staff groups first request -> adds to shipment sourcing -> marks provider confirmed.<br>4. Customer tries to withdraw confirmed request. | Unconfirmed request withdraws immediately; confirmed request cannot auto-withdraw and escalates for management review. |
| **UAT-07** | Allocation Offer & Progression | Sourcing Staff & Customer | Request linked to arrived shipment | Offer window `24h` | 1. Staff creates allocation offer for customer.<br>2. Customer views offer in `/account/product-requests` and accepts/declines. | Accepting converts/reserves stock; declining progresses waitlist to next customer. |
| **UAT-08** | Reservations, Checkout & Defect Consent | Customer & Sales Staff | Published product with `defectDisclosures` | `"UAT Sofa"` with scuff disclosure | 1. Add item to bag and open `/checkout`.<br>2. Verify **Pay** button is disabled until defect disclosure checkbox is ticked.<br>3. Tick checkbox and submit checkout.<br>4. Test second customer reserving last unit concurrently. | Checkout blocked without defect acknowledgement; 15m hold created; concurrent last-unit attempt blocked cleanly without overselling. |
| **UAT-09** | Paystack Verify & Late Payment Exception | Customer & Finance Manager | Active checkout session | Paystack test reference | 1. Complete Paystack test payment (`verifyPayment` / webhook).<br>2. Simulate expired checkout hold receiving a late webhook. | Normal payment marks order `PAID` & consumes hold; late payment after hold expiry routes to **Payment Exceptions** queue. |
| **UAT-10** | Walk-In Sale, Manual Payment & Partial Collection | Sales Clerk, Approver & Warehouse | Customer account & available stock (`2` units) | Walk-in qty `2`, Cash `GHS 7,000` | 1. Create walk-in sale (`/staff/orders`).<br>2. Record manual cash payment (`/staff/payments`).<br>3. Verify creator cannot self-approve; second approver approves.<br>4. Record collection of `1` unit, then `1` unit (`/staff/collections`).<br>5. View digital receipt. | `WALK_IN` badge shown; dual-control enforced; order moves `PARTIALLY_COLLECTED` -> `COLLECTED`; digital receipt displays line items & payments. |
| **UAT-11** | Returns, 7-Day Check & Store Credit | Customer & Manager | Collected order within 7 days | Return reason `"Frame rattle"`, Credit `GHS 500` | 1. Customer submits return on `/account/orders`.<br>2. Manager reviews on `/staff/returns`, approves with `STORE_CREDIT` (`GHS 500`) + `RESTOCK`. | Return approved; `StoreCreditEntry` (`GHS 500`) issued to customer; stock lot updated per disposition. |
| **UAT-12** | RBAC, Financial Restriction & Owner Controls | Owner & Restricted Manager | Staff user with `restrictFinancials: true` | Restricted manager account | 1. Restricted manager logs in.<br>2. Open `/staff/dashboard` and try `/staff/reports` or `/staff/expenses`.<br>3. Try exporting `AUDIT` without `audit.view`. | Financial figures hidden on dashboard; financial/unpermissioned routes blocked (`403` / permission-required screen); Owner retains full access. |
| **UAT-13** | Staff Reports & Profile Change Requests | Staff & Owner | Staff account | Anonymous report `"Bay lighting"` | 1. Staff submits anonymous report in account modal.<br>2. Staff without `staff_reports.view` cannot see `/staff/staff-reports`.<br>3. Owner resolves report as `ADDRESSED`. | Report stored with `Anonymous` author; only permissioned staff notified and able to resolve. |
| **UAT-14** | Notifications & WhatsApp Consent | Customer | Signed-in customer | Account Settings -> Communications/Notifications | 1. Update notification preferences and WhatsApp opt-in.<br>2. Trigger order update and check `/account/notifications`. | Preferences persisted (`ConsentEvent` recorded); notification appears and marks read cleanly. |
| **UAT-15** | Financial Reports & Authorized Exports | Finance Manager / Owner | Orders & expenses in period | Format `CSV`, `XLSX`, `PDF` with `from`/`to` dates | 1. Open `/staff/reports`.<br>2. Export `ORDERS` as `XLSX` and `PDF`.<br>3. Check `/staff/shipments` profitability view. | Valid `.xlsx` and `.pdf` files download; shipment profitability displays estimated cost warning; `REPORT_EXPORTED` audit logged. |
